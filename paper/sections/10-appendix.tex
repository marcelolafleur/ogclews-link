\section{The archived run record}
\label{app:run-record}
Every magnitude quoted in \Cref{sec:results} is generated from the coupled run's own
archived record, never transcribed by hand; if a source artifact is absent at
generation time, the text renders a visible \textbf{[MISSING, NOT RESOLVED]} flag
rather than a stale number. The record comprises:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{ogclews\_manifest.json} --- the authoritative statement of the applied
  channels and their realized magnitudes (price-source resolution, cost-push haircuts
  by industry, household wedge, investment delta, emissions change, mortality target,
  morbidity response), plus the \CLEWS{} case/run identifiers and the economy-model
  version;
\item \texttt{macro\_table.csv} --- the transition-path and steady-state macro
  aggregates behind \Cref{tab:phl-coupled};
\item the run's figure set, from which \Cref{fig:transition,fig:incidence} are taken.
\end{itemize}
Two quoted quantities are supplied rather than derived from the record: the employment
base behind the full-time-equivalent working-time figure (49 million employed), and
the economy-model build identity, which appears in no run artifact and is recorded
with the run's coordination notes.
