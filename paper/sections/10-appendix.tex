\section{The economy-model objects the mappings touch}
\label{app:og-equations}
The complete statement of the household problem, firm first-order conditions,
government budget, and market clearing is maintained in the \OGCore{} documentation
and is not duplicated here; this appendix fixes which objects of that statement the
mappings read or move, so the channel specifications of \Cref{sec:channels} can be
checked against the host model.

\emph{Households} choose consumption and labour over $S$ periods across $J$ types.
The mappings touch: the consumption-tax vector $\tau^{c}_{i}$, where the household
energy wedge enters the demand condition, the budget, and the composite price; the
transfer share $\alpha_T$, where recycling returns the notional revenue; the
mortality hazard $\rho(s,t)$ and the implied population, which the health mapping
recomputes under a shocked survival schedule; the effective-labour profile
$e(t,s,j)$, which the morbidity component scales on working ages; and the
subsistence floor $c_{\min,i}$, which makes energy a partial necessity.

\emph{Firms} in industry $m$ produce with private capital, public capital, and
labour. The mappings touch: the capital share $\gamma_m$ of the electricity industry
(the capital-intensity lever); the investment tax treatment inside the user cost of
capital (the capital-demand lever); and industry unit costs, where the
inter-industry cost-push applies exposure-weighted wedges.

\emph{Government} taxes, spends, transfers, and borrows. The mappings touch: the
public-investment share $\alpha_I$ (grid investment becomes public capital), the
booking of consumption-tax revenue (the wedge and the standalone carbon payment),
and, through $\alpha_T$, the transfer side of recycling.

\emph{From the solved equilibrium}, the return mappings read the portfolio return
$r_p$ (the planning-rate candidate) and industry output or household consumption
(the demand-path candidate); they write files for the energy model and change
nothing inside the economy.

\section{The archived run record}
\label{app:run-record}
Every magnitude quoted in \Cref{sec:results} is generated from the coupled run's own
archived record, never transcribed by hand; if a source artifact is absent at
generation time, the text renders a visible \textbf{[MISSING, NOT RESOLVED]} flag
rather than a stale number. The record comprises:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{ogclews\_manifest.json} --- the authoritative statement of the applied
  channels and their realized magnitudes (price-source resolution, cost-push haircuts
  by industry, household wedge, investment delta, emissions change, mortality target,
  morbidity response), plus the \CLEWS{} case/run identifiers and the economy-model
  version;
\item \texttt{macro\_table.csv} --- the transition-path and steady-state macro
  aggregates behind \Cref{tab:phl-coupled};
\item the run's figure set, from which \Cref{fig:transition,fig:incidence} are taken.
\end{itemize}
Two quoted quantities are supplied rather than derived from the record: the employment
base behind the full-time-equivalent working-time figure (48.91 million employed ---
the PSA Labour Force Survey 2024 annual average, derived from the published ratio of
5.82 million underemployed = 11.9\% of the employed), and the economy-model build
identity, which appears in no run artifact and is recorded with the run's coordination
notes.

\section{The run battery and the golden record}
\label{app:battery}
The grouped battery executes the link in its own environment and the country
\OGCore{} model in the country model's environment, exchanging files; a battery item
passes when its solve completes and its read-outs are produced. The regression record
(\texttt{results/golden.json}, v9 scenario pair) stores, for the baseline and each of
eleven experiment configurations ---
\chan{coupled}; \chan{energy\_price} (with a steady-state-only variant);
\chan{clean\_incidence}; \chan{carbon}; \chan{investment} (with a steady-state-only
variant); \chan{capital\_intensity} (with a steady-state-only variant);
\chan{health}; \chan{demand}; and \chan{discount\_rate} ---
the base and reform levels of $Y$, $C$, $K$, $L$, $r$, and $w$ at the steady state
and at transition periods $t_0$ and $t_{10}$, together with sectoral outputs $Y_m$.
The automated re-check enforces the recorded macro aggregates; the lock does not
extend to emitted artifacts or the electricity-cost path
(\Cref{sec:validation}). Two experiment names in the record predate the current
registry naming (\chan{demand}, \chan{discount\_rate} --- now the \chan{emit\_}
mappings of \Cref{app:channel-map}); the record is retained under its committed names
because it is the evidence file the v9 claims cite.
