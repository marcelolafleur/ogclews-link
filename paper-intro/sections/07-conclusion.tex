\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The OG--CLEWS link translates a solved resource strategy into the prices, investments,
health effects, and policy inputs that shape households, firms, government, and different
generations. The link then converts OG-Core activity and its equilibrium return into inputs
for the next CLEWS solve. The forward pass is implemented and tested; the monetary bridge and repeated
two-way solution remain open. Extending the same system to land and water will require new
resource-specific relationships and evidence.
