\section{What Moves Between the Models}
\label{sec:two-models}

The system contains eight relationships with three different roles. Four carry results
from CLEWS into OG-Core. Two apply policy assumptions across the models. Two carry OG-Core
results back toward CLEWS. Table~\ref{tab:two-way} summarises their economic meaning and
current status.

\toprule
Relationship & Information used & Economic role & Current status \\

The implemented sequence solves an economic baseline, reads a solved CLEWS reform, applies
the relevant changes to OG-Core, and solves the economic transition. OG-Core then writes
return and activity paths for a possible subsequent CLEWS solve. Repetition becomes
important when higher electricity costs reduce demand, or when economic conditions change
the financing of the resource plan. That outer process remains open.
