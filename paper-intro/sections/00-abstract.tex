The OG--CLEWS link currently connects energy results from the Climate, Land, Energy and
Water Systems model to OG-Core, an economy with households, industries, government, and
overlapping generations. CLEWS supplies changes in
electricity cost, grid investment, generation capital intensity, and particulate
emissions. OG-Core translates them into effects on living costs, production, public
capital, health, labour, population, and public finance. The interface can prepare a
carbon-price input for each model and defines a private-generation investment incentive
in OG-Core. The link then converts OG-Core activity and return paths into inputs for a
subsequent CLEWS solve. It preserves the economic meaning of each relationship and guards
against entering the same cost twice. The forward
CLEWS-to-OG-Core pass and a controlled CLEWS re-solve are implemented and tested. The
repeated two-way solution, the monetary-unit bridge, and parts of the policy calibration
remain incomplete.
