OG--CLEWS links the Climate, Land, Energy and Water Systems model with OG-Core to assess
the economic consequences of resource choices. Its eight channels connect costs,
investment, health and policy to household and industry responses, and feed changes in
demand and the cost of capital back into resource planning. OG-Core distinguishes households by age
and lifetime income. The combined assessment follows how costs and benefits are
distributed across the economy and across generations, and how those responses change
the resource plan.
